QUANTUM IN THE REAL WORLD

Better Materials

Could computers help us discover materials with useful properties?

Scientists search through many possible materials when looking for useful properties such as strength, conductivity, light absorption, or energy storage.

🔋 Start searching ← Applications
🔋
THE BIG QUESTION

What makes a material useful?

Different materials have different properties. A scientist might want something strong, light, conductive, heat-resistant, or able to store energy.

💪
Strength

Some materials can withstand large forces without breaking.

⚡
Conductivity

Some materials allow electricity to move through them easily.

🔋
Energy storage

Some materials can help store and release energy in batteries.

🧠 The search can become enormous

Imagine having thousands or millions of possible combinations of atoms. Which one might have the property we want?

Scientists use computers, simulations, experiments, and mathematical models to search through these possibilities.

Quantum computers are being researched as possible tools for some types of difficult material and molecular problems.

WHERE QUANTUM COMPUTING ENTERS

Why are quantum computers interesting?

🔢
Many possibilities

Material properties can depend on complicated interactions between atoms and electrons.

🟣
Quantum systems

The electrons inside materials follow quantum-mechanical rules.

🖥️
Better simulations

Researchers are investigating whether quantum computers could eventually help simulate certain quantum systems.
