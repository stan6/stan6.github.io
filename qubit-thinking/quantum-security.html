Quantum Security

Can you help Alice and Bob detect when someone tries to peek at their secret message?

📡 The Secret Message

Alice wants to send information to Bob. They use special quantum states to check whether their communication has been disturbed.

🔵

What happened to the message?

🔬 The Big Idea

In quantum communication, measuring a quantum state can change it. This means that attempts to observe certain quantum information can sometimes be detected.

Scientists study these ideas as part of quantum cryptography.

💡 Think like a scientist

If looking at something changes it, could that help us notice when somebody is trying to listen to a secret conversation?
