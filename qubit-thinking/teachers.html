LEARNING GOALS

What will students explore?

The activities intentionally introduce ideas through experimentation before introducing technical vocabulary.

01

Information

Students discover that computers can represent information using simple states such as 0 and 1.

02

Possibilities

Students distinguish between what is known, what is possible and what can be predicted.

03

Probability

Students use repeated experiments to notice patterns in probability.

04

Qubits

Students encounter the basic idea of quantum information through a visual, interactive model.

05

Measurement

Students explore how measurement produces an observable result.

06

Computational Thinking

Students practice prediction, experimentation, pattern recognition and problem solving.

Questions to ask students

🔮

🎯

🟣

TEACHING TIPS

Let students discover first.

1. Predict

Ask students what they think will happen before they press a button.

2. Experiment

Let them run the activity several times rather than explaining everything first.

3. Discuss

Ask students why the result surprised them or matched their prediction.

4. Connect

Connect the activity back to probability, computation and quantum information.
