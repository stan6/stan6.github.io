✨ Superposition

A qubit can represent a quantum combination of possible states.

One qubit, many possibilities

A classical bit is either 0 or 1.

A qubit can be prepared in a superposition of 0 and 1.

🧠 Qubit Thinking:

Don't ask only: "Which answer is it?"

Ask:
"What possibilities can I explore?"

A simple example is:

|ψ⟩ = (|0⟩ + |1⟩) / √2

This equal superposition gives a 50% probability of observing 0 and a 50% probability of observing 1 when measured in the computational basis.
