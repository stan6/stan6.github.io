QUANTUM IN THE REAL WORLD

Quantum Chemistry

How could quantum computers help us understand molecules?

Molecules follow the rules of quantum physics. That makes them fascinating — and sometimes very difficult for ordinary computers to model.

🧪 Start the experiment ← Applications
🧪
FIRST QUESTION

Why are molecules interesting?

Everything around us is made from atoms and molecules. Water, oxygen, medicines, food, and even the materials in your phone all involve molecules.

⚛️
Atoms

Atoms are tiny building blocks that can join together to form molecules.

🧬
Molecules

Molecules are groups of atoms connected in particular arrangements.

⚡
Energy

Molecules can exist in different energy states. Understanding these states is important in chemistry.

🧠 The big idea

Electrons in molecules behave according to quantum mechanics. Predicting exactly how all those electrons behave can become very difficult as molecules get larger.

Researchers are investigating whether quantum computers could eventually help simulate some of these quantum systems.
